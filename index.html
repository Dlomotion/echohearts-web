The world

A bond with a living world.

Echohearts: Rebearth is an original world where restoration begins with understanding. As a Frequency Tamer / Core-Binder, the player’s connection with Eco-Kin is central to the journey—not a shortcut around the life and history of each individual.

The A.E.G.I.S. is the physical bracer and bond interface. The Anima-Link preserves a two-way connection, with consequences that matter to both player and Eco-Kin.

Core attributes

  • VibranceVitality, recovery capacity, and life-force expression.
  • DensityPhysical presence, impact, resistance, and structural strength.
  • HarmonyCoordination, relationship stability, and cooperative recovery.
  • PurityStability, cleansing, and resistance to corruption.
Designed with care

Connection over collection.

These pillars describe the intended experience. They are design goals, not claims that the corresponding game systems are already playable.

01 / BOND

Anima-Link

A bi-directional bond: strain and harm are not one-sided abstractions. The connection has consequences for the player and their Eco-Kin.

Design principle
02 / INTERFACE

A.E.G.I.S.

A physical bracer envisioned for scanning, Echoprints, anomaly and material tracking, restoration diagnostics, and Echo Egg care readouts.

Design target
03 / HOME

Sanctuary

A place for building, growing, water, care, power, logistics, and nursery work—planned as a connected part of world restoration.

Design target
Guiding principles

Care shapes every choice.

These principles describe project direction, not a claim that finished game systems are available.

01 / CARE

Respect each life

Eco-Kin are autonomous partners. Care, safety, and voluntary choices come before collection.

02 / CONSEQUENCES

Connection matters

The Anima-Link is bi-directional; its strain and damage consequences must matter to both sides.

03 / RESTORATION

Repair, don't reset

Restoration should respond to the living world and preserve the memory of what happened.

04 / EVIDENCE

Share honestly

Design intent, browser presentation, and verified game behavior are distinct kinds of evidence.

Care, not chance

Every beginning deserves care.

Echo Eggs are developmental and incubation items—not capture devices, random species generators, or guaranteed rarity containers.

Aero

Maintain airflow and stable pressure.

Aura

Favor calm, low-stress Harmony care.

Glaze

Keep cold and moisture conditions stable.

Pyre

Use controlled warmth with thermal venting.

Radiant

Provide clean light and Purity-oriented care.

Shade

Keep conditions dim and disturbance low.

Terra

Provide a mineral bed and vibration stability.

Tide

Support humidity and clean-flow care.

Verdant

Use living soil and protective plant cover.

Volt

Keep charge controlled and safely grounded.

Resonant

Maintain a neutral, stable pre-resolution shell.

Sanctuary

Reflect a high-quality care state, not a species or tier.

Prismatic

Hold multiple signatures; outcomes remain eligible identities only.

Ancient

Preserve provenance without implying Legendary status.

Sovereign

Event-grade shell; ordinary incubation excludes protected identities.

These are examples from the Echo Egg design registry. Care and lineage rules—not shell appearance—guide the system. A Blight-Stressed Egg is a rescue condition, never a rarity reward.
