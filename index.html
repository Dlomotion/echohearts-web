The world

A bond with a living world.

Echohearts: Rebearth is an original world where restoration begins with understanding. As a Frequency Tamer / Core-Binder, the player’s connection with Eco-Kin is central to the journey—not a shortcut around the life and history of each individual.

The A.E.G.I.S. is the physical bracer and bond interface. The Anima-Link preserves a two-way connection, with consequences that matter to both player and Eco-Kin.

Connection over collection.

These pillars describe the intended experience. They are design goals, not claims that the corresponding game systems are already playable.

Anima-Link

Care, not chance

Every beginning deserves care.

Echo Eggs are developmental and incubation items—not capture devices, random species generators, or guaranteed rarity containers.

Aero

Airflow and pressure care

Terra

Mineral bed and vibration stability

Verdant

Living soil and plant cover

Radiant

Clean-light and Purity care

These are examples from the Echo Egg design registry. Care and lineage rules—not shell appearance—guide the system. A Blight-Stressed Egg is a rescue condition, never a rarity reward.
